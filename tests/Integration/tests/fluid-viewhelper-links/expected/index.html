Fluid ViewHelpers 

A ViewHelper the reference documents, as a tag: <f:format.html>{record.bodytext}</f:format.html> , as a closing tag: </f:format.html> , as an inline call: {f:translate(key: 'title')} or {f:uri.image} , and by its name alone: be:moduleLink .

A ViewHelper the reference does not document: <my:custom.thing> .

Fluid that names no ViewHelper: {page.uid} .
